# Brand voice

verify → link/connect your card → spend → earn cashback. Never “card activation.”

## Tone
Clear, warm. Dignity and ease. Prefer “you don’t have to ask.”

## Hard rules
- Never sell/share personal info claims that contradict privacy page
- No DoD endorsement
- No invented savings %
- Mirror awkward-ask testimonials when useful
- SD only
